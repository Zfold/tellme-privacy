TellME
Beta Tester Feedback Guide
Version 1.0.0 · Closed Beta · May 2026
Thank you for testing Tell ME! Your feedback directly shapes this app before public release. Please complete the tasks below and answer the feedback questions honestly — critical feedback is more valuable than praise. Testing takes approximately 20-30 minutes.
Part 1 — First Impressions
Complete these before doing anything else in the app.
1
Welcome Screen
  • Open Tell ME for the first time
  • Read the welcome screen
  • Tap "Start Exploring"
FEEDBACK

Was the welcome screen clear about what the app does?

Did you feel excited to try it, or confused about what to do?

Anything missing from the welcome screen?

2
Home Screen
  • Look at the home screen layout
  • Note the location toggle, scan count, and buttons
FEEDBACK

Is the home screen intuitive? Do you know what to do next?

Does the dark/gold design appeal to you?

Part 2 — Scanning & Identification
Test the core feature with at least 5 different subjects.
3
Take a Photo — Landmark or Building
  • Tap "Take Photo" and photograph a recognizable building, monument, or landmark
  • Wait for the result screen
  • Read all the information provided
FEEDBACK

Was the identification correct?

Was the information interesting and accurate?

How long did the scan take? Was the wait acceptable?

What confidence percentage was shown?

4
Scan a Plant or Flower
  • Find a plant, flower, or tree near you
  • Photograph it up close
  • Review the result
FEEDBACK

Was the plant identified correctly? If not, what was it actually?

Was the safety banner shown if applicable?

Was the growing/care information useful?

5
Scan Food or a Product
  • Photograph a packaged food item, a dish, or a branded product
  • Make sure any text/labels are visible
  • Review the result
FEEDBACK

Was the product or food correctly identified?

Did Tell ME read the text/brand name on the packaging?

Was the information relevant and interesting?

6
Scan an Insect, Animal, or Bird
  • Photograph any wildlife — a bug, spider, bird, pet, or even a photo of an animal from a book or screen
  • Review the result
FEEDBACK

Was the species identified correctly?

Were safety warnings shown for spiders or other hazardous creatures?

Did the information teach you something new?

7
Choose from Gallery
  • Tap "Choose from Gallery" instead of taking a new photo
  • Select any interesting photo from your camera roll
  • Review the result
FEEDBACK

Did the gallery picker work smoothly?

Was the identification accurate from an existing photo?

8
Try "Not Quite Right?"
  • Find a scan where the confidence is below 85%
  • If you see the "Not quite right? Try again →" button, tap it
  • Compare the original and alternative identifications
FEEDBACK

Did the alternative identification make more sense?

Were the Original/Alternative tabs easy to understand?

Part 3 — Follow-Up Questions
Test the "Ask Your Guide" feature.
9
Ask a Follow-Up Question
  • On any result screen, tap one of the suggested follow-up questions
  • Then type your own question in the chat box
  • Try at least 2 questions on one scan
FEEDBACK

Were the suggested questions relevant?

Were the answers informative and conversational (not robotic)?

Did the chat show normal text or raw code/JSON?

Part 4 — Travel Journal
Test saving, organizing, and sharing discoveries.
10
Save to Journal
  • On a result screen, tap "Save"
  • Review the suggested collections
  • Select collections or create a new one
  • Tap "Save to Journal"
FEEDBACK

Were the suggested collections logical?

Was the save process intuitive?

Could you create a custom collection easily?

11
Browse Journal & Collections
  • Tap the Journal button on the home screen
  • Switch between Collections and All Entries tabs
  • Open a collection and browse entries
  • Tap an entry to see the full detail view
  • Tap the image to view it full-screen
FEEDBACK

Is the journal organized well?

Did the full-screen image viewer work?

Would you actually use this as a travel journal?

12
Share a Collection
  • Open a collection with at least 2 saved entries
  • Tap the Share button
  • Select specific entries (not all)
  • Tap Share to generate the PDF
  • Send the PDF to yourself via email or messages
  • Open the PDF and review it
FEEDBACK

Did the entry selection work intuitively?

Did the PDF generate and share successfully?

Did images appear in the PDF?

Was the PDF layout professional and readable?

Would you share this with friends or family?

13
Delete an Entry
  • Delete one entry from your journal
  • Confirm the deletion in the popup
FEEDBACK

Was the confirmation dialog clear?

Did the entry disappear immediately?
