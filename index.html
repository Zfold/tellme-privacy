TellME — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Overview

Tell ME ("the App") is an AI-powered visual identification and information companion developed by Steve Grant ("we," "us," or "our"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Tell ME is designed to provide you with rich, contextual information about the world around you while collecting the minimum data necessary to function.

Information We Collect

Photos and Images: When you use Tell ME to scan a subject, your photo is sent to our servers for processing. Images are transmitted securely via HTTPS, processed by Google Cloud Vision API and Anthropic Claude AI to generate identification and information, and then immediately discarded. We do not store, retain, or archive any images on our servers. Images exist in server memory only for the few seconds required to process your request.

Location Data (Optional): If you enable the location feature, Tell ME accesses your device GPS coordinates to improve identification accuracy. Location data is used in real-time to provide context (such as identifying local plant species or nearby landmarks) and is not stored on our servers. You can disable location access at any time using the toggle on the home screen or through your device settings.

Journal Data: When you save discoveries to your Travel Journal, the identification results, location information, and image reference are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Usage Data: We track daily scan counts on your device to manage the free tier limit. This count resets daily and is stored locally on your device only.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identity information
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles
  • We do not track your browsing history or app usage patterns
  • We do not use cookies or tracking pixels
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • We do not store your photos on any server

Third-Party Services

Tell ME uses the following third-party services to provide its identification and information features:

  • Google Cloud Vision API: Processes images for visual identification, text detection, and landmark recognition. Images are sent securely and processed according to Google Cloud's Privacy Notice.
  • Anthropic Claude AI: Generates detailed information and expert-level content about identified subjects. Data is processed according to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: Converts GPS coordinates to human-readable location names (reverse geocoding). No personal data is shared — only coordinates.

These services receive image data and/or location coordinates solely for the purpose of processing your identification request. We do not share any additional personal information with these services.

Data Security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. API keys and credentials are stored securely on our server infrastructure and are never embedded in the App itself. Your journal data is stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's built-in security features (screen lock, encryption).

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Delete individual journal entries: Open any entry in your Travel Journal and tap the Delete button. This permanently removes the entry and its associated data from your device.
  • Delete entire collections: Open any collection and use the delete option to remove the collection and all its entries.
  • Delete all app data: Go to your device Settings → Apps → Tell ME → Storage → Clear Data. This removes all journal entries, collections, settings, and scan history from your device.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling Tell ME removes all locally stored data from your device.

Since Tell ME does not store any user data on external servers, there is no server-side data to delete. All your data exists only on your device and is fully within your control.

If you have any concerns about your data or wish to confirm that no data is retained, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Tell ME is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App's target audience is users aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to address the situation.

Content Safety

Tell ME includes automated content filtering powered by Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch to prevent the processing of explicit or violent imagery. The App also includes safety warnings for potentially hazardous subjects such as poisonous mushrooms, venomous spiders, and toxic plants. Medical observations include disclaimers directing users to consult healthcare professionals.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
